# Explanation-direction intake (intent-walk dispatch — Phase 14 stub)

The **explanation** Diataxis direction is understanding-oriented: it gives
background and rationale so a reader grasps *why* something is the way it is. In
dogen, an explanation unit is drafted from a recorded **intent walk** — a guided
"5 whys" that draws out the reasoning the page must carry. That walk is the
subject of **Phase 15**; this file is the honest Phase-14 dispatch stub (D-05).

## Honest hard-stop: this cycle stops before declaring

The intent-walk mode's dispatch is real — a declared explanation direction lands
here. But its **body is not built yet**: the intent walk that draws out and
records the steering an explanation draft needs arrives in **Phase 15**. Until
then, no explanation unit can reach drafting, because there is no recorded intent
steering for a draft to stand on.

So this cycle the dispatch **stops before declaring**. Do not run
`dogen unit new --intent explanation` and do not sign anything off — an
explanation unit declared now would have no walk to feed it and could not be
drafted honestly.

## What to do at the stop

Mirror the courtesy of the retired reference-only hard-block: explain the
situation plainly and offer to note the intent for the next cycle, then STOP.

> What you're describing reads as an **explanation** — understanding-oriented,
> "help me understand why". dogen routes explanation units through an intent walk

> ready to pick up then, and stop here — or, if there's a genuine reference,
> tutorial, or how-to surface underneath, reframe it as one of those and declare

**Phase 15 owns the walk body.** This file is a deliberate scaffold, not a gap:
when the intent walk ships, its mode body replaces this stub at the same load
point. Until then the only correct outcome for an explanation direction is
note-the-intent-and-stop (D-05).
